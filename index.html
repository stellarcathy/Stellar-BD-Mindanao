Weekly Report No. 001 • Week 25 | June 18–19, 2026

Prepared by Cathy Lyn B. Paraluman and Azelle J. Sulla • Stellar Seeds Corporation

Executive Summary

The Business Development Team conducted visits to potential partners and customers within South Cotabato and General Santos City. The objective was to introduce Stellar Seeds Corporation's seed processing, warehousing, fumigation coordination, and related services while exploring potential business opportunities and strategic partnerships.

Objectives

  • Introduce Stellar Seeds Corporation and its services.
  • Explore business opportunities in seed processing, storage, and logistics.
  • Build relationships with key industry stakeholders.
  • Identify strategic partnerships and future collaboration opportunities.

Companies Visited

1. CPF – Charoen Pokphand Foods

54F5+8C7, Polomolok, South Cotabato

Status: Initial Contact Established

Marketing materials delivered for endorsement to the Administration Department. Follow-up communication is required.

2. Dole Philippines Incorporated

Cannery Rd, Cannery Site, Polomolok, South Cotabato

Status: Opportunity Currently Not Required

Dole utilizes drying processes for papaya seeds but indicated that the services offered by Stellar are not currently required.

3. One Harvest Enterprises

National Highway, Brgy Silway 7 – Klinan 6 Rd, Polomolok

Status: High Potential Opportunity

Interest identified for drying services for corn, coffee, and cassava. Peak corn volume was reported at approximately 1,000 MT/month, with about 200 MT/month during lean season.

Next: Prepare formal proposal and quotation and conduct technical assessment.

4. General Santos Feed Manufacturing Corporation

Glamang, Polomolok, South Cotabato

Status: Relationship Established – No Immediate Opportunity

Processes fresh whole corn plants at approximately 35 MT/day for feed ingredients exported to Korea. Current process does not require drying of corn grain.

5. ADM Philippines

Glamang, Polomolok, South Cotabato

Status: Initial Contact Established

Company brochures and business cards were endorsed for future reference. Follow-up with the appropriate department is required.

6. Vitarich Corporation

General Santos City

Status: Referral Obtained – Follow-up Required

The team was referred to the main office and plant operations in Panacan, Davao City for processing, storage, and logistics discussions.

7. Alsons Aquaculture Corporation

Maribulan, Alabel, Sarangani Province

Status: Initial Contact Established

Marketing materials were endorsed through the facility reception process. Further communication is required.

8. San Miguel Foods, Inc.

Lagao, General Santos City

Status: Guidance Obtained – Follow-up Required

Future discussions should be directed to the San Miguel Plant in Santa Cruz, Davao City, with formal email coordination before a plant visit.

9. Cargill Philippines

Tambler, General Santos City

Status: Initial Contact Established

Marketing materials were endorsed to the appropriate department. Follow-up communication is required to identify potential collaboration.

10. AMG Corporation

Lagao, General Santos City

Status: Qualified Service Provider Identified

Cold storage facility assessed for Advanta Seeds. Rooms were reported at 125–170 tons per room, with blast freezing, inventory management, handling, forklift rental, and refrigerated container support.

Key Opportunities Identified

🟢 One Harvest Enterprises

Potential drying services for corn, coffee, and cassava. Formal proposal and quotation requested with positive feedback from management and ownership.

🟢 AMG Corporation

Potential cold storage provider for Advanta Seeds. Commercial proposal and storage rates were obtained and the facility was assessed for suitability.

Challenges Encountered

  • Limited availability of some key decision-makers during visits.
  • Additional information and technical presentations may be required.
  • Further assessment is needed to determine alignment between customer requirements and available services.
